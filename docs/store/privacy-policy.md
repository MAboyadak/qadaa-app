# Privacy Policy: Qadaa (قضاء)

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

Qadaa is a free app, offered as sadaqah jariyah, that helps you estimate, plan and track making up missed prayers.

## Summary
**We collect no data.** The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Everything you enter stays on your device.

## What the app stores, on your device only
- Your estimate inputs (date of birth, age at puberty, periods of missed prayers and, for women, cycle and nifas days).
- Your progress log (each make-up prayer you record).
- Your settings (language, pace, reminder times, chosen city or approximate location).

This data is kept in a local database inside the app. It is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Location
If you choose "Use my location", the app reads your **approximate** location once to calculate prayer times on your device. The location is stored only on your device and is never transmitted. You can choose a city from a list instead.

## Notifications
Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push service is used.

## Backups
If you export a backup, the app creates a file and opens your phone's share sheet. Where you send that file (for example Google Drive or WhatsApp) is your choice and is governed by that service's policy.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.
